# Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

## Definition
Hypertension is defined as a sustained systolic blood pressure ≥ 130 mmHg or diastolic blood pressure ≥ 80 mmHg (ACC/AHA 2017 guidelines).

## Classification
| Stage | Systolic (mmHg) | Diastolic (mmHg) |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | < 120 | < 80 |
| Elevated | 120–129 | < 80 |
| Stage 1 | 130–139 | 80–89 |
| Stage 2 | ≥ 140 | ≥ 90 |
| Hypertensive Crisis | > 180 | > 120 |

## First-line pharmacotherapy
- **Thiazide diuretics** (e.g., hydrochlorothiazide 12.5–25 mg/day)
- **ACE inhibitors** (e.g., lisinopril 10–40 mg/day) — avoid in pregnancy
- **ARBs** (e.g., losartan 50–100 mg/day) — preferred in ACE-I intolerant patients
- **Calcium channel blockers** (e.g., amlodipine 5–10 mg/day)

## Lifestyle modifications
- DASH diet (reduce sodium < 2.3 g/day)
- Regular aerobic exercise ≥ 150 min/week
- Weight loss if BMI > 25
- Limit alcohol: ≤ 2 drinks/day (men), ≤ 1 drink/day (women)
- Smoking cessation

## Monitoring
- Re-check BP at 1 month after initiating therapy
- Target BP < 130/80 mmHg in most adults
- Annual lab panel: BMP, CBC, lipid panel, urinalysis
