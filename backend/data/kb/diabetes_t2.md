# Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

## Diagnostic criteria (ADA)
- Fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL (confirmed on repeat)
- 2-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during 75 g OGTT
- HbA1c ≥ 6.5%
- Random plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL with classic symptoms

## Glycemic targets
- HbA1c < 7% for most non-pregnant adults
- HbA1c < 8% for older adults with limited life expectancy or high hypoglycemia risk
- Fasting glucose 80–130 mg/dL
- Post-prandial glucose < 180 mg/dL

## Pharmacotherapy — step therapy
1. **Metformin** 500 mg BID with meals (titrate to 1000 mg BID) — first-line
2. Add **GLP-1 agonist** (e.g., semaglutide, liraglutide) if CV disease or obesity
3. Add **SGLT-2 inhibitor** (e.g., empagliflozin) if heart failure or CKD
4. Add **DPP-4 inhibitor** (e.g., sitagliptin) if low hypoglycemia risk preferred
5. **Insulin** (basal: glargine or detemir) when oral agents insufficient

## Monitoring
- HbA1c every 3 months until stable, then every 6 months
- Annual: comprehensive foot exam, dilated eye exam, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio
- Blood pressure target: < 130/80 mmHg
- Statin therapy for patients ≥ 40 years

## Hypoglycemia management
- Mild: 15 g fast-acting carbs (glucose tablets, 4 oz juice); recheck in 15 min
- Severe (unconscious): glucagon IM/IN or IV dextrose
